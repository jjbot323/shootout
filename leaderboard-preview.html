What B gains

  • Position, with ties. Four players tied on level par read as T5 rather than as rows 5 through 8, which is how a golfer expects to read a board.
  • Red means under par at a glance, the way every televised leaderboard works. Today both figures are the same weight.
  • Team on the row. The colour chip already hints at it; the label removes the guess, and matters here because the whole event is teams.
  • Rows about a third shorter, so all sixteen fit a phone screen with no scrolling.

What it costs

  • Six columns on a 375px phone means either a squeeze or sideways scrolling. The Team column is the obvious one to drop, since the chip already carries it.
  • The green header is Augusta's, not this event's. Shown here as-is for fidelity; in the site's palette it would be the blue.
  • "Today" doesn't apply. The masters board splits round score from tournament total; a one-round shootout has only one, so I mapped that column to Gross.

If you want B, say so and I'll wire it in — most likely dropping the Team column on narrow screens and recolouring the header band to the site's blue, unless you would rather keep the green.
